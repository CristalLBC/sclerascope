{% elif kind == 'privacy' %}

What we collect

  • Two eye photographs per scan — the images you take in the App.
  • The analysis output — the structured observations and your generated report.
  • Consent record — timestamp, waiver version, IP address, browser string, and the optional name and email you enter. This exists so there is a record that you agreed to the waiver.
  • Purchase data — the code used to unlock a report.

How the photos are used

Your photos are sent over HTTPS to our analysis provider (Google's Gemini API) purely to produce your report. They are stored on our server only so your report can be displayed, and they are deleted automatically after 30 days. Reports are also removed on the same schedule unless you ask us to keep them.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your photos, your report, or your contact details.
  • We do not use your photos to train models.
  • We do not post anything to social media.
  • We do not share data with advertisers.

Deletion

Email {{ support }} with the scan ID shown on your report and we will delete the photos and the report. Deletion is normally completed within 7 days.

Security

Transport is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to stored scans is restricted to the operator. No system is perfectly secure; do not upload images you are not comfortable transmitting.

Children

The App is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
